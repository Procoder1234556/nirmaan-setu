# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Oil India project-control planners, field supervisors, and construction leaders coordinating remote pipeline and capital-project work.

## Product Purpose

Nirmaan Setu turns field progress into schedule-aware decisions by connecting site observations to Primavera activities and verified CPM updates.

## Positioning

It combines offline-first field capture, confidence-routed activity matching, human review, and in-process CPM recalculation in one project-controls workspace.

## Operating Context

Users ingest Primavera P6 XER or MS Project XML baselines, record field updates from remote sites, review ambiguous links, and inspect schedule impact and historical benchmarks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The application is a Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL web portal. Schedule changes must be traceable and only verified actuals affect CPM. Voice transcription is optional and requires a server-side GROQ API key.

## Brand Commitments

Nirmaan Setu (निर्माण सेतु) is an Oil India Limited SIH26122 project. The established product identity uses amber, navy, and emerald accents.

## Evidence on Hand

The workspace has live routes for projects, CPM schedule views, reviewer queue, field logs, and historical benchmarks. Example project data is demonstrative; no customer claims or external benchmarks are added to the landing page.

## Product Principles

- Field evidence must remain attributable and reviewable.
- Uncertainty is routed to people, not silently written into a schedule.
- Schedule insight should follow operational reality.
- Remote work must remain viable when connectivity is intermittent.
